# Anvil: The Beginner's Guide

> **Live App**: [https://anvilapp.online](https://anvilapp.online)

New to Anvil? Welcome! This guide is here to walk you through everything step-by-step, just like a friend showing you around. 

Anvil turns your daily habits into an adventure. Instead of just ticking boxes on a boring checklist, you'll earn experience points (XP), collect gold coins, level up your character, challenge friends to duels, and unlock smart insights that help you understand your habits better.

Here is how everything works and how to get the most out of your journey.

---

## 1. Creating Your First Habit

Once you sign in at [https://anvilapp.online](https://anvilapp.online), head over to the **Habits** page or click the **"+"** button to add your first habit.

When setting up a habit, you'll give it a few details:
- **Name**: What you want to do (e.g., *Drink 2L Water*, *Read 10 Pages*, or *30 Min Workout*).
- **Class**: Think of this as the theme or energy of the habit:
  - **Warrior** (Strength & Physical Health): Workouts, walking, stretching, sports.
  - **Mage** (Intelligence & Mental Focus): Studying, reading, writing, coding.
  - **Rogue** (Wisdom & Agility): Meditation, journaling, waking up early, organizing.
- **Difficulty**: Choose between **Novice** (easy), **Adept** (medium), or **Master** (hard). Harder habits reward you with more XP and coins when completed!
- **Scheduled Days**: Pick which days of the week you plan to do this habit (like every weekday or only weekends).

---

## 2. Checking Off a Habit & Earning Rewards

Whenever you complete a habit for the day:
1. Open your dashboard or the month calendar view.
2. Click the checkbox next to the habit for today's date.
3. A quick prompt will ask you what time of day you completed it (*Morning*, *Afternoon*, *Evening*, or *Night*). You can also add a short note (like a mini journal entry).

### What happens when you check it off?
- **XP**: You immediately earn Experience Points toward your next level.
- **Coins**: You earn gold coins that you can spend in the shop.
- **Streak**: Your streak count increases by +1 day.
- **Stat Boost**: Your character's attributes (Strength, Intelligence, or Wisdom) go up based on the habit's class.

---

## 3. Streaks & Streak Protection

Your **Streak** is the number of consecutive days you've completed your scheduled habits. Watching that number climb from 3 days to 30 days is one of the most satisfying parts of building a habit.

### What breaks a streak?
If you have habits scheduled for a day and you don't complete them before midnight, your streak will reset to zero.

### How to protect your streak:
- **Streak Freeze**: You can buy a Streak Freeze in the shop using your earned coins. If life gets busy or you're sick and miss a day, your Streak Freeze will automatically kick in and save your streak from breaking.
- **Streak Shield**: As you level up and maintain long streaks, you can unlock shield charges that give you extra breathing room.

---

## 4. Levels and Experience (XP)

Every single check-off gives you XP. As your XP bar fills up, you **Level Up**!

- Everyone starts at **Level 1**.
- Higher levels unlock special profile titles, exclusive shop items, and prestige on the leaderboard.
- Harder habits (Master tier) give larger XP payouts, helping you level up faster.

---

## 5. Your Character Attributes (Class Radar)

On your profile, you'll see a spider-web chart called the **Class Radar**. This shows how balanced your life habits are across four attributes:
- **STR (Strength)**: Built by completing Warrior habits (fitness, exercise, health).
- **INT (Intelligence)**: Built by completing Mage habits (studying, learning, reading).
- **WIS (Wisdom)**: Built by completing Rogue habits (mindfulness, reflection, planning).
- **CHA (Charisma)**: Built through overall consistency, winning rival duels, and balanced days.

You can focus heavily on one class to become a specialized powerhouse, or balance all four to become an all-around hero.

---

## 6. The Shop

As you complete habits, you'll accumulate gold coins. You can spend them in **The Shop** on fun cosmetics and helpful items:
- **Themes**: Change the visual colors of the entire website (like *Cyberpunk*, *Midnight Mode*, or *Forest Cloak*).
- **Streak Freezes**: Buy backup protection so you never lose a hard-earned streak.
- **Cosmetics**: Unlock custom items and badges for your profile.

---

## 7. Change the App's "Flavor" (Terminology Themes)

Not into fantasy or RPG words? You don't have to use them! Anvil has four built-in terminology flavors that change the language across the whole app:

- **RPG Mode** *(Default)*: Quests, XP, Bosses, Duels, and Classes.
- **Racing Theme**: Laps, Horsepower, Pit Stops, and Rivals.
- **Sports Theme**: Drills, Conditioning, Matchups, and Training.
- **Plain / Minimalist**: Clean, modern language with simple terms like *Habits*, *Points*, *Streaks*, and *Categories*.

You can switch flavors anytime in your **Settings** or the **Shop**.

## 8. Quest Chains (Habit Stacking)

Have you ever noticed that it's easier to do a habit when it's attached to another habit? For example, drinking a glass of water immediately after brushing your teeth.

In Anvil, you can link 2 or more habits together into a **Quest Chain**:
- Example Morning Routine: *Make Bed -> 10 Min Stretch -> Drink Water*.
- If you complete every habit in the chain on the same day, you unlock a **Chain Bonus** with extra XP and special chain-mastery achievements.

---

## 9. The Rival System (1v1 Habit Duels)

Friendly competition is one of the best motivators. The **Rival System** lets you challenge other users to direct 1-on-1 habit duels:

1. Go to the **Rivals** page and click **Challenge**.
2. Pick an opponent (or invite a friend), select the habit you both want to track, and set the duration (7 or 14 days).
3. Both of you go about your normal week, checking off your habit.
4. Whoever logs more completions by the end of the duel wins the match!
5. The winner earns coins, climbing rank on the rivalry board, and leaves a victory message on the duel summary.

## 13. Smart Behavioral & AI Insights

Anvil includes several built-in tools that analyze your past 30 days of check-offs to help you understand your habits without judging you:

### Momentum Score
Streaks can feel brutal—if you have a 30-day streak and miss one day, a normal app makes you feel like you failed completely. Anvil's **Momentum Score** measures your overall consistency (0 to 100) based on your recent activity:
- **On Fire (80–100)**: Maximum momentum! You are crushing your daily habits.
- **Building (60–79)**: Strong, steady progress and climbing toward personal bests.
- **Slipping (40–59)**: Some habits are starting to lag—time for a focused boost.
- **Fading (20–39)**: Consistency is dropping; complete a simple habit today to turn it around.
- **Cold (0–19)**: Inactivity warning—use a Streak Freeze or complete an easy habit to reignite your momentum!

Missing a single day only slightly lowers your score; you can quickly build it right back up the next day.

### Procrastination Fingerprint
On your profile, this card shows when you tend to do your habits:
- **Average Execution Window**: Tells you what time of day you usually get your habits done on average.
- **Late-Night Execution Rate**: Shows the percentage of tasks you check off late at night (after 10:00 PM). If this number is high, it means you might be putting things off until the last minute.
- **Avoidance Pattern**: Detects if you tend to swap one type of task for another on busy days (for example, if you consistently skip your study habits on days you do your workout).

### Habit Autopsy (AI Coach)
If a habit is stalling or you keep skipping it, you can request a **Habit Autopsy**:
- Powered by **Google Gemini 3.6 Flash** (`gemini-3.6-flash`), Anvil looks at your recent completion trends, timing, and difficulty.
- It gives you a short, honest paragraph explaining *why* you might be struggling with that specific habit, plus one clear, practical tip to fix it.

### Adaptive Difficulty
If you've been acing a habit easily for weeks, Anvil will gently suggest leveling it up from *Novice* to *Adept*. On the other hand, if a habit has been consistently missed, the app will offer to scale it back so you can rebuild your confidence without burning out.

### Month Forecast
At the top of your monthly calendar, this tool predicts your estimated month-end completion percentage based on your current pace, helping you stay ahead of your goals before the month ends.

## Quick Tips for Success

1. **Start Small**: Begin with 2–3 easy habits. It's better to build a 10-day streak on easy tasks than to burn out on 10 hard ones.
2. **Use Quest Chains**: Group your morning or evening habits together so they become a single automatic routine.
3. **Keep a Streak Freeze in Your Pocket**: Buy a freeze from the shop as soon as you have 100 coins. It's the best insurance policy for busy days!

Have fun, level up, and enjoy forging habits that last! If you have any questions or feedback, reach out or visit [https://anvilapp.online](https://anvilapp.online).
